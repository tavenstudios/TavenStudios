Built around possibility.

TAVEN STUDIOS is an independent creative technology initiative exploring software, AI and digital experiences. It is small on purpose, and focused on learning, experimenting and building a portfolio.

Our vision

Useful, beautiful digital experiences that start as honest experiments and grow through feedback and curiosity.

Our philosophy

Design and engineering belong together. A good idea deserves craft, and craft deserves clear thinking.

Our approach

Prototype early, show the work as it is, and say plainly what is finished and what is still an idea.

Our values

Curiosity, honesty, restraint and respect for the people we learn from and build with.
